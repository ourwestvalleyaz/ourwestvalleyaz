{{< tolleson-surveillance-glance >}}

Tolleson City records document the placement of license plate readers and budget allocations for automated license plate reader (ALPR) cameras and drone-related programs. This page distinguishes documented deployment from budgeted projects and identifies details that the reviewed records do not establish.

## License plate readers

### Documented FY2020 deployment

Tolleson's FY2020 Comprehensive Annual Financial Report states that license plate readers were strategically placed throughout the city during fiscal year 2020.

The report does not identify the manufacturer, number of readers, precise installation dates or locations, or whether the same equipment remains in operation.

### FY2021 and FY2022 budget entries

| Fiscal year | City budget description | Budget allocation |
|---|---|---:|
| FY2021 | ALPR Cameras (License Plate Project) | $50,000 |
| FY2022 | ALPR Cameras (License Plate Project) | $60,000 |

Each fiscal year's Capital Improvement Plan and adopted budget repeat the same project entry. They are not separate allocations within that year.

These entries document budgeted amounts, **not confirmed purchases or expenditures**. The reviewed records do not establish whether the FY2022 entry represented additional equipment, a continuation of the earlier project, or replacement equipment.

## Drone budget history

Tolleson budget documents separately identify a Drone Project, a FEMA Drone Program (FEMA stands for Federal Emergency Management Agency), and Public Safety Police drone entries. The reviewed records do not establish the FEMA Drone Program’s funding source, operational purpose, or relationship to the separately listed police drone entries.

| Fiscal year | Budget description | Budget allocation |
|---|---|---:|
| FY2020 | Drone Project | $10,000 |
| FY2023 | FEMA Drone Program | $167,000 |
| FY2025 | FEMA Drone Program equipment | $63,620 |
| FY2026 | FEMA Drone Program equipment | $63,620 |
| FY2026 | Public Safety Police – Drone | $35,000 |
| FY2027 | FEMA Drone Program equipment | $63,620 |
| FY2027 | Public Safety Police – First Responder Drone | $65,000 |
| FY2027 | Public Safety Police – Drone | $40,000 |

These are budget entries, not confirmed purchases or cumulative spending. Repeated FEMA Drone Program entries across fiscal years should not be added together as though they represent separate purchases. The FEMA Drone Program and police drone entries are presented separately because the reviewed records do not establish that they describe the same equipment or program.

## What the reviewed records do not establish

The reviewed records do not establish:

- Tolleson's current ALPR camera count or complete equipment inventory.
- The manufacturer or vendor of the FY2020 ALPR equipment or later ALPR projects.
- Whether the FY2021 and FY2022 ALPR allocations resulted in purchases, expansion, or replacement.
- The complete actual expenditure history for ALPR and drone equipment.
- The current ALPR data-retention period, access controls, or data-sharing arrangements.
- The current operational status, equipment count, or vendor of the drone-related programs.

An unidentified detail should not be interpreted as evidence that the equipment, policy, or arrangement does not exist.
