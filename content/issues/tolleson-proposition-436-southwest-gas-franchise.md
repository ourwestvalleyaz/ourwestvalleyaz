## What a yes or no vote meant

{{< ballot-comparison >}}

{{< ballot-choice vote="yes" >}}

**Approved the Southwest Gas Corporation franchise.**

- Authorized a franchise agreement with Southwest Gas Corporation.
- Allowed use of City rights-of-way for gas utility purposes.
- The City described the proposed term as up to **25 years**.
- The October 2020 City notice identified the term as beginning November 4, 2020 and ending November 4, 2045.

{{< /ballot-choice >}}

{{< ballot-choice vote="no" >}}

**Did not approve the proposed franchise.**

- The proposed franchise agreement would not have been approved through Proposition 436.
- Southwest Gas would not have received the proposed franchise authorization described in the ballot materials.

## Why Proposition 436 was on the ballot

Proposition 436 concerned renewal of a franchise agreement with Southwest Gas Corporation.

The City described the franchise as authorization for the use of City rights-of-way for gas utility purposes.

## What Proposition 436 would change

The proposed franchise would provide Southwest Gas Corporation with the franchise authorization described by the City for use of City rights-of-way.

The City's October 2020 election notice described the agreement as a 25-year term beginning November 4, 2020 and ending November 4, 2045.

## Timeline

**October 1, 2020**  
The City published information about Proposition 436 and the other two Tolleson propositions on the November general-election ballot.

**November 3, 2020**  
Proposition 436 appeared on the City of Tolleson's general-election ballot.

**November 4, 2020**  
The City identified this date as the beginning of the proposed 25-year franchise term.

**November 13, 2020**  
Maricopa County's final official results recorded 1,602 YES votes and 413 NO votes for Proposition 436.

**November 4, 2045**  
The City identified this date as the end of the proposed franchise term.
