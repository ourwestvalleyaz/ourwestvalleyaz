## What a yes or no vote meant

{{< ballot-comparison >}}

{{< ballot-choice vote="yes" >}}

**Approved the issuance of general obligation bonds.**

- Authorized up to **$21,000,000** in general obligation bonds.
- The City stated that the bonds would fund parks and recreation projects, including an aquatic facility.
- The bonds would be repaid through secondary property taxes.
- The City stated that issuing the bonds would result in a property-tax increase sufficient to pay annual debt service.

{{< /ballot-choice >}}

{{< ballot-choice vote="no" >}}

**Did not authorize the proposed general obligation bonds.**

- The proposed $21 million bond issuance would not have been authorized.
- The proposed parks and recreation projects would not have received funding from these bonds.
- The proposed secondary-property-tax debt service associated with the bonds would not have been established.

{{< /ballot-choice >}}

## Why Proposition 435 was on the ballot

Proposition 435 asked Tolleson voters to authorize the issuance and sale of $21 million in general obligation bonds.

The City described the proposed bonds as funding for parks and recreation projects, including an aquatic facility.

## What Proposition 435 would change

Approval would authorize the City to issue the proposed general obligation bonds.

The City's October 2020 election information stated that the bonds would be repaid through secondary property taxes and that issuing the bonds would result in a property-tax increase sufficient to pay the annual debt service.

**October 1, 2020**  
The City published information about Proposition 435 and the other two Tolleson propositions on the November general-election ballot.
