Our West Valley AZ is an independent, noncommercial civic-information project
for Arizona's West Valley. It is not affiliated with the City of Avondale or
any other government agency.

## Why OWVAZ exists

Useful local-government information is often spread across city websites,
budgets, meeting portals, agendas, reports, and other public records. OWVAZ
organizes that information around what residents are trying to find and
explains it in plain language.

**Find it. Understand it. Go to the source if you want more.**

## How information is researched

OWVAZ takes accuracy seriously and prefers authoritative primary sources such
as official government websites, adopted budgets, agendas, minutes,
resolutions, contracts, and public records.

Important factual claims should be traceable to identifiable evidence.
Summaries and explanations are intended to make source material easier to
understand, not to replace the underlying records. When an official source
or service is available, OWVAZ links residents directly to it.

When available evidence does not establish something, OWVAZ aims to identify
that limitation rather than fill the gap with an assumption.

## Editorial independence

OWVAZ is independently operated. It is not operated by or on behalf of a
city, government agency, political candidate, campaign, political party, or
government contractor.

The project is noncommercial and is intended to help residents find,
understand, and verify information about their communities.

## Use of artificial intelligence

OWVAZ may use artificial intelligence tools to assist with organizing,
comparing, summarizing, researching, and presenting information. AI tools
may also assist with technical work involved in developing and maintaining
the website.

AI output is not treated as an authoritative source. Factual claims
published by OWVAZ are expected to be supported by underlying records or
other identifiable sources, and important claims should be verified against
those sources whenever practical.

## Corrections

Accuracy is an ongoing responsibility. When reliable information shows that
OWVAZ has published a factual error or that important context is missing,
OWVAZ aims to correct or clarify the information.

Residents are encouraged to provide corrections and supporting sources
through the [Contact page](/contact/).

## Privacy

OWVAZ is designed so residents can use the site without being tracked or
profiled by OWVAZ. The current site does not require resident accounts and is
designed without advertising, tracking pixels, behavioral analytics, browser
or device fingerprinting, or third-party social trackers. Site searches run
in the resident's browser.

[Read the OWVAZ Privacy page](/privacy/) for details about search, cookies,
browser storage, external links, and the site's privacy-by-design approach.
