Timeline

City-reported activity and budget allocations are shown separately. Budget entries are not confirmed expenditures.

  1. FY2020 · Reported deployment

    The City's FY2020 financial report states that license plate readers were strategically placed throughout Tolleson. The report does not identify the manufacturer or number of readers.

  2. FY2020 · Drone budget

    A City budget entry identifies $10,000 for a Drone Project. The allocation does not establish a purchase or deployment.

  3. FY2021 · ALPR budget

    The adopted budget lists $50,000 for ALPR Cameras (License Plate Project).

  4. FY2022 · ALPR budget

    The adopted budget lists $60,000 for the same named ALPR project. The reviewed records do not establish whether this was a continuation, expansion, or replacement.

  5. FY2023 · FEMA Drone Program

    The adopted budget lists a $167,000 FEMA Drone Program allocation.

  6. FY2025 · FEMA Drone Program

    The adopted budget lists $63,620 for FEMA Drone Program equipment.

  7. FY2026 · Drone budgets

    The adopted budget lists $63,620 for FEMA Drone Program equipment and a separate $35,000 Public Safety Police drone entry.

  8. FY2027 · Drone budgets

    The adopted budget lists $63,620 for FEMA Drone Program equipment, $65,000 for a First Responder Drone, and a separate $40,000 Public Safety Police drone entry.

Repeated annual FEMA Drone Program allocations should not be added together as cumulative spending.
