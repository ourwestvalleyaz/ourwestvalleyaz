What the alert counts mean

The reports describe alerts involving stolen vehicles, stolen license plates, wanted persons, missing persons, warrants, and other activation types.

An alert is not necessarily a unique vehicle, an arrest, or a confirmed match. A vehicle can generate multiple alerts, and the reports document instances where officers could not locate the vehicle.
