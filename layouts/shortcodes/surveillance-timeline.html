Timeline

  1. Around 2008

    A historical City budget documents an early Avondale license plate reader deployment, training, stored plate records, and approximately 50,000 plates collected.

  2. FY2022

    The City documents a police drone program and seven licensed drone pilots.

  3. FY2023

    The City reports a license plate reader program that identified two stolen vehicles and the addition of two police drones.

  4. FY2025

    The City describes testing intersection cameras functioning as license plate readers.

  5. FY2025 activity reported in the FY2026 budget

    Avondale reports assigning a full-time dispatcher to the West Valley Real-Time Crime Center.

  6. FY2026

    Police asset-replacement planning includes drones and a license plate reader among multiple categories of equipment.

  7. FY2027 recommended budget

    The City documents 15 existing LPR cameras, requests 58 additional cameras, describes access to cameras shared by more than 140 law-enforcement agencies, describes 16 police drones, proposes a Drone as First Responder program, and requests funding for Police Data Sharing.

  8. FY2027 tentative budget

    The tentative budget continues to list the 58-camera LPR request, Police Data Sharing, Public Safety Drone Program, body-worn-camera redaction software, and other Police Department supplemental requests.

  9. FY2027 final budget adopted

    On June 1, 2026, the Avondale City Council adopted the $514.3 million FY2027 budget through Resolution 1041-0626.

    The final budget includes $239,000 in State RICO funding assigned to Information Technology and separately identifies a budgeted line item for cameras and drone equipment, services, and warranty. The final resolution does not identify the earlier 58-camera LPR proposal by name.
