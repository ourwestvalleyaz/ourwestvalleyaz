How to read these figures

FY2022 is reported actual spending. FY2023 shows an annual budget and a separate projected actual amount. FY2024 is an initial budget request. These are not four contract prices.

The budget comments identify the expense as license plate reader cameras and state that the FY2024 budget reflects actual cost.

The reviewed public sources do not establish the current annual cost, renewal amount, or contract expiration date.
