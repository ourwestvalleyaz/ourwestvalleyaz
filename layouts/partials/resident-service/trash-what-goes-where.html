Disposal guide

What goes where?

Common household items at a glance. Use the City links for unusual materials, special handling and complete requirements.

Trash

Black container

Put in trash

  • Bagged household garbage
  • Non-recyclable household waste
  • Other non-hazardous regular household trash

Keep out

  • Household hazardous waste
  • Electronics
  • Bulk-trash items
  • Recyclable materials

Bag and tie regular trash and keep the container lid closed.

Recycling

Blue container · loose items only

Commonly accepted

  • Paper and flattened cardboard
  • Plastic bottles and containers
  • Aluminum and metal cans
  • Glass bottles and jars

Keep out

  • Plastic bags and film
  • Food and liquids
  • Garden hoses and cords
  • Clothing and textiles

Empty and rinse containers and break down cardboard boxes.

Full City recycling rules →

Bulk trash

Curbside · maximum 3 cubic yards

Commonly accepted

  • Furniture and mattresses
  • Stoves, washers, dryers and water heaters
  • Tree branches, brush and palm fronds
  • Bagged grass, leaves and loose landscape debris

Keep out

  • Household hazardous waste
  • Batteries and liquid paint
  • Construction and demolition debris
  • Refrigerators, freezers and refrigerant appliances
  • Recyclable cardboard, paper and containers
  • Animal, livestock or agricultural waste

Branches must be 4 feet or shorter and 6 inches or less in diameter.
